# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0). In short:

## Our pledge

We want participation to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Be precise about security claims and say how you know. "I could not verify this" is a useful contribution.
- Keep reports about vulnerabilities private until they are fixed (see `SECURITY.md`).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, discrimination, doxxing, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Requests for, or contributions of, offensive tooling: working exploits, malware, or help attacking systems you do
  not own or have permission to test. This is a defensive project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behavior they consider inappropriate. Report concerns
privately to the maintainers via a GitHub private security advisory on this repository or by contacting the
repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
